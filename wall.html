This is a wall in my house. It used to be painted green for some reason, until we repained our house. Come to think of it, our house's walls were weird. The walls were maroon and dark green and stuff; super odd. Really glad we painted over it. That small section of wall also definitely has a hollow space behind it, which you can tell by hitting the side of the wall and hearing the empty noise. I don't think there's a room back there. Secret room, perhaps? That would be neat, but is very unlikely. It's probably just some useless space under the stairs.

But what if it isn't?

What if there really is a secret room behind that wall? What if there's a secret treasure behind the wall? What if there's a big balloon behind the wall? Okay, the balloon one isn't the most exciting, come to think of it. But I still like the treasure idea. Actually, a balloon that's managed to stay inflated since the house's construction would be very neat.
