Mildly Sketchy Battery


Back to Portfolio

For some context: I like to collect old tech. Old cameras, computers, laptops, all that good stuff. One day, I found a really great deal on a Thinkpad T450s on eBay. I was super excited when it arrived. I quickly took it upstairs to begin some quick tinkering before I had to leave the house for something important and with no parent home to lecture me about being late, what could go wrong?
DEFINITELY NOT FORESHADOWING

Anyway, I open up the laptop and almost fell asleep right then and there because great googly moogly there was a big pillow in it. A pillow made of lithium ion. Keep in mind, this laptop had just been rocking around in a bunch of hot mail trucks and sitting on the 100° porch, so it could have blown at any moment. Again, from my dedfinite lack of foreshadowing, I was about to leave the house and no one would be home, So I was really stuck between a rock and a hard place.

I did what I could. I Called my dad, did some research, and hatched a plan. I cut open a sandbag, poured the contents into a big pot, then gently placed the spicy li-ion pillows onto the sand. I placed the pot in the middle of a big concrete area (JUUUUST in case it popped while I was gone), then noticed the metal pot was making a very concerning magnification of sunlight onto the inner rim, so I grabbed a nearby planter and set it up in such a way to both avoid a flaming planter and a battery hotspot. Thankfully, my house didn't blow up and I still have the battery.

Okay, maybe that last part isn't too exciting.
